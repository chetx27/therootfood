# TheRootFood

Farm products catalog platform for B2B and B2C ordering.

## Project Overview

TheRootFood is a catalog-based e-commerce site showcasing premium agricultural products including stone-ground gluten-free ragi flour, vetiver root products, free-range eggs, goat meat, country chicken, baked goods, and goat milk soap.

## Product Categories

- Flours & Grains (stone-ground, gluten-free)
- Herbal Products (vetiver roots and powders)
- Free-range Proteins (eggs, goat meat, country chicken)
- Artisanal Baked Goods (cakes, tea cakes)
- Natural Personal Care (goat milk soap)

## Business Model

Direct-contact ordering system. Catalog-first interface with minimal, clean design. No checkout flow—buyers initiate orders via direct contact channels.

## Design Approach

Minimal UI informed by agricultural commerce best practices. Focus on product clarity and direct customer engagement without heavy animations or flashy elements.
